Comments to author (Associate Editor)
=====================================

Two reviews were collected for the paper. The reviewers
concur in saying that the paper has moderate quality and
limited relevance to the CCTA scope. 

In particular, there is a lack of clearly defined
requirements, which weakens the verification and makes the
evaluation largely subjective. The novelty with respect to
existing work is not sufficiently clarified, and the stated
objectives are not fully supported by the validation.

Finally, the results are based solely on simulations.
