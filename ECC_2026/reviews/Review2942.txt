Reviewer 20 of CCTA 2026 submission 136

Comments to the author
======================

The paper addresses an interesting and practically relevant
problem, and the DFL-based approach is technically sound in
its general structure. However, several issues need to be
addressed before publication.

Major Issues

The central motivation claims to avoid stall risks and
minimum airspeed constraints, but stall is never modeled
and the aerodynamic model is only valid well above stall
speed. The main selling point of the paper is never
actually demonstrated.

The mimicry proof in Section III.B relies on Assumption 2
which is essentially circular: it assumes the framework
works where it works, without ever formally characterizing
the set of feasible trajectories over which the result
holds.


Minor Issues

Control inputs are never shown in the validation.

No quantitative metrics are reported (e.g., RMSE, maximum
tracking error). Validation relies entirely on visual
inspection of plots.
