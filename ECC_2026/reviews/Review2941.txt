Reviewer 19 of CCTA 2026 submission 136

Comments to the author
======================

The paper proposes a control strategy for a multicopter
equipped with a two-axis gimbal based on emulation of the
flight dynamics of a fixed-wing aircraft. The control
design is based on a state-input mapping between the two
aircraft types that is derived based on feedback
linearization. Overall the approach is interesting but not
so surprising or novel. Indeed the core issues on
singularities and input saturation that are usually
encountered in feedback linearization approaches for such
problems, are not mitigated here but are rather tackled
through the convenient Assumptions 1 and 2. Based on this
and the fact that the results are only simulation based, it
might be the case that the approach needs further
elaboration, either theoretically or experimentally or
both, before reaching publication status.
